Abitur Tracker

The real app, running inside a model iPhone. The grades here are examples. Your own grades stay in the app on your phone.

  • HistoryQ3: a bad Klausur (06). The sheet shows which "other" average gets you the next point.
  • GoalSwitch between 1.3 and 1.2 and watch the needed average change.
  • StrikeOpen any GK subject and flip "Strike" on a semester.
  • SettingsThe gear: subjects, exam roles, "Taken" per semester.
